# 📊 Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard
## 📌 Project Overview
The **Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard** is an interactive business intelligence project designed to analyze sales performance across multiple business dimensions.
The dashboard provides a consolidated view of key business metrics, including revenue, profit, orders, discounts, cost of goods sold (COGS), and gross profit margin.
It enables stakeholders to monitor business performance, identify revenue-generating product categories, compare sales channels, and understand geographical sales distribution through interactive filters and visualizations.
## 🎯 Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales and financial performance.
- Analyze revenue and profitability across different product categories.
- Identify high-performing countries and geographical markets.
- Compare the profitability of different sales channels.
- Understand customer purchasing segments.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Support data-driven business decision-making.

## 📊 Dashboard Features & Analysis
### 1. Monthly Sales Performance
- Monthly revenue trends.
- Monthly gross profit trends.
- Identification of performance fluctuations throughout the year.
### 2. Country-Wise Revenue Analysis
- Comparison of revenue across countries.
- Identification of major revenue-contributing markets.
### 3. Sales Channel Profitability
- Comparison of profit generated by different sales channels.
- Evaluation of channel-wise business performance.
### 4. Product Category Analysis
- Profit contribution across product categories.
- Category-level comparison of revenue and profitability.
### 5. Customer Segmentation
- Revenue analysis across Business, New, and Returning customers.
- Comparison of customer segment contributions.
### 6. Product-Wise Revenue Analysis
- Comparison of revenue generated by individual products.
- Identification of high-revenue products.
### 7. Interactive Filtering
Users can dynamically filter dashboard results using:
- Quarter
- Country
- Product Category
- Month
